按结构与用途选取代表图，去除同类配色、数据集及历史变体。数据来源逐图标注；收录不等于完整 CP 验收。<br>One representative per visual subtype; color-only, dataset-only and historical variants are omitted. Inclusion does not certify scientific fidelity.
