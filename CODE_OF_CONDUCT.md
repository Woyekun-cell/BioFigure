# 社区行为准则 / Community code of conduct

BioFigure 社区欢迎不同研究领域、经验层级和技术背景的参与者。交流应围绕证据与作品，保持尊重、具体和可执行。<br>
The BioFigure community welcomes contributors across research fields, experience levels, and technical backgrounds. Keep discussion respectful, evidence-based, specific, and actionable.

可以直接指出科学、统计、代码或视觉问题；请解释影响并给出可核查依据。禁止人身攻击、骚扰、歧视、威胁、泄露私人信息以及明知无权仍传播受限材料。<br>
Direct criticism of scientific, statistical, code, or visual problems is welcome when the impact and verifiable basis are stated. Personal attacks, harassment, discrimination, threats, disclosure of private information, and knowing redistribution of restricted material are prohibited.

维护者可以编辑、隐藏或删除违反准则的内容，并限制持续违规者参与。安全或隐私事件请勿公开附加敏感材料；先通过仓库所有者的 GitHub 主页联系维护者。<br>
Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content that violates this code and may restrict repeated violations. For safety or privacy incidents, do not attach sensitive material publicly; contact the maintainer through the repository owner's GitHub profile first.

本准则参考 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)，并针对科研数据与来源完整性作了简化。<br>
This code is adapted in concise form from the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) and adds boundaries for research data and source integrity.
